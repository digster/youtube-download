## Safari cookie permissions troubleshooting

- User reported identical download failures for every video. The screenshot's
  Errno 1 on Safari Cookies.binarycookies identifies macOS privacy restrictions
  during cookie extraction. The upstream yt-dlp issue #7392 reports the same
  failure; Apple documents permissions in System Settings → Privacy & Security.
- Shelf's server exited while checking its parent process, so the launching app
  could not be confirmed. Guidance: allow the app/terminal that starts Shelf,
  quit/reopen it and restart Shelf, then Resume / retry. Alternatives: another
  signed-in browser or No cookies for public videos. No permissions or cookie
  contents were accessed or changed during diagnosis.
